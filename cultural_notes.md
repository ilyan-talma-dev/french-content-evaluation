# Cultural Context Notes

Example 1

Phrase:
Métro, boulot, dodo.

Literal translation:
Metro, work, sleep.

Meaning:
A repetitive daily routine.

Explanation:
This expression reflects French cultural commentary on routine lifestyle.
Understanding cultural context is essential when evaluating language output.


Example 2

Phrase:
Chercher midi à quatorze heures.

Literal translation:
To look for noon at 2 PM.

Meaning:
To complicate something simple.

Explanation:
This idiom illustrates how literal translation fails without cultural understanding.
Language evaluation must consider cultural meaning rather than word-by-word interpretation.
