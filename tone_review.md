# Tone Evaluation Examples

Example 1

Sentence:
Ce n'est pas terrible.

Literal meaning:
It is not terrible.

Actual tone:
Negative / disappointed

Explanation:
Although the sentence seems neutral literally, in French it usually implies dissatisfaction.
Tone interpretation is essential when reviewing language output.


Example 2

Sentence:
C'est pas mal du tout.

Literal meaning:
It's not bad at all.

Actual tone:
Positive

Explanation:
This phrase is often used to express approval. Without tone awareness, AI systems may misinterpret it as neutral.
